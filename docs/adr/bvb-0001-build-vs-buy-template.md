## Decision

State the weighted winner, the option id, and the weighted score. State in the same paragraph whether that option is also the lowest 3-year operating total.

The tool writes the file. A person accepts it by changing Status. Generating the file does not buy, provision, or contact a vendor.

## What would have to change to flip the decision

List the smallest one-at-a-time weight change, after renormalising the others, that produces a new winner. Include winner stability and full rank stability on the ±0.05 / ±0.10 grid.

## Exit and revisit triggers

- Revisit on or before: DATE (suggest six months from the case `as_of`)
- Revisit sooner when a re-run shows:
  - The weighted winner changed
  - Winner stability fell below the floor recorded for this case
  - A realized price increase exceeded `annual_price_increase`
  - Build hours exceeded the overrun scenario
  - The with-exit cash ranking and the weighted ranking diverged by more than the modeled exit cost

Exit trigger for a buy choice: failed export drill, price above the assumption, or a change in the data-use terms that the rationale described.

Exit trigger for a build choice: the team that owns the eval set is gone, or time-to-value slipped past the revisit above.

## Consequences

- The record is this ADR plus the YAML. Both stay in version control.
- Rollback is reverting the YAML and regenerating the ADR. The previous ADR remains in history.
- No vendor is contacted by generating the file.
